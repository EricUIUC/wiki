# MATH231

[MATH241 (Calculus III)](../Course%20Wiki/MATH%20Course%20Offerings/MATH241.md) is the third course in the calculus sequence that is required for all students in enrolled in the college of Grainger.

## Who Should Take the Exam

For students who want to take [MATH285](../Course%20Wiki/MATH%20Course%20Offerings/MATH285.md) and [ECE210](../Course%20Wiki/ECE%20Course%20Offerings/ECE210.md) early, it is crucial to get credit for MATH241 as early as possible. These 2 courses are prerequisites themselves for most of the 300 level ECE courses, hence it is advisable to take the proficiency test if you have the means to do so.

It's not an easy proficiency exam, and does require some preparation. This test is mainly for those who either had Multivariable classes in high school, either via the school's own extended curriculum or collaboration progarms with community colleges, or for those who wish to speed things up and challenge themselves. However, like all proficiency exams, there is no penalty for failing the MATH231 proficiency exam, so if you're on the fence, take the exam.

## Content Covered

- Vectors and Geometry of Space
- Dot and Cross product
- Chain rule
- Vectors Functions
- Partial Derivatives
- Double and Triple integrals
- Integration in different coordinates
- Line integrals
- Green and Stoke's theorm
- Curl, Divergence, and gradiant

## Exam Format

As of 2024, the math proficiency exams are taken in person. The exam consists of multiple multiple choice and free response questions. The exam duration is 3 hours, but the exam is designed to take 1.5-2 hours on average.

## How to Prepare

[The official syllabus list](https://math.illinois.edu/resources/syllabus-math-241) has all the information you need regarding the content covered for the exam. Here you can details on the official textbook used for the course. The textbook contains many practice questions that are similar in difficulty to the proficiency exam, so it is highly recommended to acquire an online or physical copy of the textbook if you can. If you know friends who have taken MATH241, it is highly recommended to ask them for their midterm papers which are good practice for the proficiency exam. [Khan Academy's](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/multivariable-calculus) Multivaribale calculus course is also highly valuable, though it does miss out on some different parts of the course

## Life After

If you pass the MATH241 proficiency exam, you have a solid understanding in integration techniques calculus in different dimensions. To make the most of your credit in MATH231, take MATH285 and ECE210 as soon as possible. Doing so will speed up your ECE journey, allowing yourself more semesters to take interesting ECE electives or even graduate early.

If you don't pass the MATH241 proficiency exam, don't be disheartened: the exam is considered a challenging one, and there is a wide range of content covered. Your efforts to study MATH241 material before the semester will still pay off, and the course will be easier for you than students who are learning those concepts for the first time. It will also give you an edge when it comes to the aforementioned postrequisite courses.
